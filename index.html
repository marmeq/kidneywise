Privacy Policy

Kidneywise: Renal Diet Tracker · Last updated November 8, 2026

Kidneywise is a food and nutrient diary for people who follow a kidney diet. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Data you enter

Your food log, fluid entries, limits, body weight, recipes, custom foods and lab results are stored only on your device in the app's local database. We do not operate servers that receive this data, there are no accounts, and we cannot see your entries. If you share a report or CSV file, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal or health data. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no tracking.

Camera and photos

When you scan a nutrition label or ingredient list, the text is read on your device with Apple's Vision framework. The image is not saved or uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Kidneywise is a diary. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it does not interpret lab results. Use the limits your doctor or renal dietitian gives you. Nutrient values come from USDA FoodData Central (public domain) and are averages; packaged foods vary.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Support & contact
